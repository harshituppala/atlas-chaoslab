# Ready to record

The release has two entry points: a free public sample and the full local workbench. The private workbench is not an always-online hosted service. No new recurring hosting or model budget is needed for these recordings.

## Before opening the recorder

Use only the fictional files in `datasets/documents`. Close AWS, account settings and private contract tabs. Connect to the local workbench off camera; do not record a token, login form, password manager, terminal environment or private configuration file. Keep the browser at a readable zoom with enough space to show a requirement and its evidence together.

## ATLAS — about three minutes

Start with the problem: a buyer asks for recovery within four hours; the supplier promises eight. Open the local workbench and upload the fictional PDF and DOCX. Run the offline baseline, then open the recovery requirement and both exact source passages. Download the original PDF to show that the finding is tied to a retained document.

Reject the conflicting finding with a short reason. Reload and reconnect to reopen the saved decision, then show its audit entry. Explain that the tool selects evidence and preserves a review; the person remains responsible for the conclusion. End by opening the free public sample, where visitors can try the fictional task without uploads or an account. Its session history is temporary.

## CHAOSLAB — about three minutes

Open the recorded evaluation page and inspect one wrong answer with a valid citation. Explain why matching a source does not make the conclusion correct. Show the separate frozen contract test: 123 contracts, 77.5% correct labels, and 49.4% correct labels with complete evidence. Show the contradiction errors and missing predictions too.

Switch to the independently installed four-answer example. Run the correct comparison, then the wrong decision, broken citation and missing attempt. The first command succeeds; the other three fail the gate. These four answers are deliberate controls, not fresh model calls. The complete commands are in the [example](../examples/review-regression/README.md).

## What to leave out

Do not describe the public sample as accepting real uploads, the displayed model results as live generation, the contradiction problem as solved, or the local recovery tests as deployed cloud acceptance. Keep detailed infrastructure evidence in the repository rather than trying to read it all on screen.

The [walkthrough](demo-walkthrough.md) has the longer sequence and reproducible rehearsal commands. Recordings themselves are the next presentation step; none is claimed to exist yet.
